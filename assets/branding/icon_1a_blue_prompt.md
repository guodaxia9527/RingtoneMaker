# 1A 白底蓝色图标

制作方式：内置 image_gen 图像编辑工具。

输入：`icon_1a_white.png`。输出：`icon_1a_white_blue.png`。

保留原有白底、构图和两侧绿色音波，将剪刀和中央三根实线音波改为编辑器手柄的蓝色方向（#087EFF）。Android 各密度资源由该输出缩放生成。

Keep both dashed vertical columns and ALL outer waveform bars their original lime green color. Keep clean white separation between scissors and waveform, and white holes in the scissors. Background must remain pure white #FFFFFF throughout. Crisp flat colors, no texture, no gradients, no shadows, no outlines, no text, no new elements. Do not redesign or rearrange anything. Return one square production icon.
